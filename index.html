Questions

How do you price a job?

By how much space your stuff takes up in the truck. Labor, loading and disposal are included. Send a photo and we'll give you a price before we start.

What kinds of jobs do you take?

All kinds. Homes, apartments, offices, commercial spaces, rentals and job sites. If it needs to go, we'll take a look.

How soon can you come?

Once we agree on a price, we can typically come within the business week, depending on our appointment schedule.

What happens to my stuff?

We dispose of or relocate everything in a safe and convenient manner, so you don't have to think about it.

How do I pay?

Payment is flexible. We accept cash, credit card and Venmo.

Where do you work?

We're based in Johns Creek and serve anywhere in the Metro Atlanta area.
